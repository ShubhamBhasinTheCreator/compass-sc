## The problem
When something goes wrong at USC — late financial aid, a lost USCard, a landlord who won't fix the heat — students don't know which office handles it, what to do first, or what the deadline is. The myUSC links page alone lists dozens of portals and services.

## What Compass SC does
- **For students:** describe your problem in plain words. Compass's AI pipeline identifies the issue, matches the right USC office, and gives you a clear action card: steps in order, deadlines, and every link verified against official usc.edu pages. It writes up your case so you can submit it to the office or send it yourself.
- **For offices (pilot view):** cases arrive organized and complete, with status tracking the student can follow.
- **For USG (USG Pulse):** anonymized reports are grouped into themes and ranked by how many students they affect, with spike alerts and an AI-written weekly brief, so USG can fix problems at the root.

## Privacy
Personal details are removed before anything reaches USG. USG sees only themes, counts and anonymized quotes. Offices see a student's details only if that student chooses to submit their case.
